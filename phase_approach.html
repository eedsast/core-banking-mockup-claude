Core Banking Platform

Phased Delivery Approach

Platform is structured for incremental adoption. Phase I delivers the complete core banking foundation — platform infrastructure, accounts and ledger, lending, payments, cards, client onboarding, digital signing and notifications. Phase II extends the platform with advanced and optional capabilities once the core is live and stable.

📦 Phase I · Core Platform 🚀 Phase II · Extensions ⛔ Out of Scope
Phase I — operational core, delivered and live together
Phase II — extension modules activated after core go-live
Out of Scope — not part of this platform delivery
I
Phase

Core Banking Foundation

All Phase I modules form the non-negotiable operational core. They are deployed first and go live together, providing a complete end-to-end banking platform from day one. Accounts and deposits ship together in this phase — the potential split is a Phase II item.
