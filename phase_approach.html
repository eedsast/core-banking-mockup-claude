OneFor · Core Banking Platform

Phased Delivery Approach

OneFor is structured for incremental adoption. Phase I delivers the complete core banking foundation — accounts, loans, payments, cards, digital signing and reporting. Phase II extends the platform with advanced and optional capabilities once the core is live and stable.

📦 Phase I · Core Platform ✂️ Phase I · Split Candidates 🚀 Phase II · Extensions ⛔ Out of Scope
Phase I — delivered as single service, potentially split in Phase II
Phase I — likely split into separate microservices in Phase II
Phase II — optional extension modules activated after core go-live
Out of Scope — not part of this platform delivery

Phase I · Split Candidates

These modules ship as Phase I services but are architected to split into finer-grained microservices in Phase II as load and complexity grow.

✂️ These services are deployed as single bounded contexts in Phase I. Their internal domain complexity makes them natural candidates for decomposition as the platform scales — each boundary is pre-defined to make the split a structural refactoring, not a redesign.
🔐
Authentication & Authorization
auth-service-boundaries
Token-based auth, RBAC and session management — may split into Auth and Permissions services in Phase II
🏦
Accounts & Deposits
accounts-deposits-service-boundaries
Current, savings and deposit accounts — product configuration may split to a separate Deposits service
